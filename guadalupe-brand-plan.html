⚠
What gets cut from the original plan
The gradual reveal sequence — one room per week — is now compressed into the first 10 days post-launch. The priority list build phase is happening in parallel with active market exposure, not before it. This means you’re generating leads and content simultaneously rather than warming an audience first. That’s fine — the renderings are strong enough to do both jobs at once. The tradeoff is that the first few weeks will be high-effort and you need to be highly responsive. The content calendar below reflects this compression.

04 — Content Pillars
Six pillars.
One consistent story.
Every post belongs to one of six pillars. This keeps the feed purposeful, varied, and on-brand — and makes content planning systematic.
01
Architecture & Exterior
20% of feed
The building is the anchor. The facade, street presence, aerial views, and material details communicate permanence before a single interior is shown.
  • Street-level facade at dusk
  • Aerial overview of the site
  • Rear elevation + garage detail
  • Construction progress shots
  • Side-by-side: neighborhood context vs. The Row
02
Interior Spaces
25% of feed
The kitchen, living room, master suite, and baths — each a standalone feature reveal. One room. One image. One sentence of copy.
  • Kitchen / living / dining reveal
  • Master bath: freestanding tub + gold fixtures
  • Master suite with marble fireplace
  • Bedroom + floor-to-ceiling windows
  • Option 1 vs Option 2 design comparison
03
The Rooftop Experience
20% of feed
The sauna, cold plunge, terrace, and skyline view are the most emotionally compelling content in the project. This pillar sells the lifestyle, not the specs.
  • Sauna with cedar interior + skyline view
  • Cold plunge at golden hour
  • Rooftop dining setup with Pittsburgh backdrop
  • Night terrace — city lights
  • Morning: coffee on the terrace
04
Pittsburgh as Context
15% of feed
The skyline is not decoration — it's the context. Posts that anchor the project to the city, its culture, and its story give non-Pittsburgh buyers a reason to care.
  • Skyline from the rooftop terrace
  • "The skyline is not the view. It is the context."
  • Mexican War Streets neighborhood walk
  • Walking distance: restaurants, institutions, culture
  • Historic block + modern contrast
05
The Details
10% of feed
Close-cropped material shots reward the eye. Brass hardware, marble grain, oak millwork — the images that stop the scroll and signal that nothing was incidental.
  • Brass floor-mounted faucet close-up
  • Marble fireplace surround detail
  • White oak cabinetry + brass pulls
  • Herringbone marble floor with brass inlay
  • Steel window frames against limestone
06
The Story
10% of feed
The development journey, the vision, and the people behind the project. This pillar builds trust and turns followers into invested stakeholders before the presale opens.
  • Site before + rendering after
  • Why Guadalupe Place (the street's story)
  • The decision to include a sauna on every unit
  • Design process: choosing the materials
  • Meet the developer / agent

07 — Presale Strategy
From follower
to reservation holder.
The funnel is five stages. Each stage has a distinct action, a distinct message, and a distinct definition of success.
01
Awareness
Discover The Row on Guadalupe
Instagram content, paid social, personal page cross-posts, PR, and word-of-mouth drive initial discovery. The teaser video already started this. Goal: brand recognition and emotional connection before any price or sales conversation.
02
Interest
Follow + Join Priority List
A follow converts a viewer to an audience. An email signup converts a follower to a lead. Website visits, DM inquiries, and email list signups are the key KPIs here. The priority list creates exclusivity — "you'll hear before the public does."
03
Consideration
Private Preview + Floor Plans
Qualified leads receive a private preview invitation — virtual walkthrough or on-site tour. Floor plans and specification packages are shared privately, not publicly. This meeting is where the sale happens. The preferred lender intro happens here too.
04
Decision
Reservation Deposit
Reservation holds the unit. Refundable deposit (suggest $5K–$10K) demonstrates intent and protects both parties. Financing pre-approval through preferred lender runs in parallel. The reservation converts to a PSA once financing is confirmed.
05
Purchase
PSA Execution + Construction Financing
All four end-buyer PSAs executed enables construction financing through the preferred partner lender. This is the commercial goal. Each signed PSA is a milestone — announce in brand voice on Instagram to reinforce scarcity and momentum for remaining units.
Scarcity Framing (Brand-Faithful)
There are genuinely only 4 homes. This is real scarcity — never manufactured. The brand voice handles it with restraint:

"One home has found its owner. Three remain."

Not: "ONLY 3 LEFT — ACT NOW!!" — that voice doesn't belong here and it never will.
Preferred Lender Integration
The preferred lender is a marketing asset, not just a finance tool. Position it as: "We've done the work for you — a lender who understands new construction and has already underwritten this project." Feature them in one Instagram post and on the /reserve page. Their pre-approval process becomes the natural next step after every private preview.
