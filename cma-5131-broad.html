Garfield is a sharply bifurcated market — a fully renovated 3-bedroom and an un-renovated 3-bedroom can sell more than $250,000 apart. 5131 Broad was gut-renovated in 2022, placing it firmly in the upper, renovated tier. The only comps that matter for pricing are other renovated sales.


Two anchors shape the recommendation: (1) this home listed at $435K in 2022, was reduced repeatedly, and sold for $340K — a signal that this specific layout and block has historically priced below raw $/sqft. (2) The renovated comps have since established a $402K–$425K band, anchored by 4815 Dearborn — a near-identical renovated 3-bed that sold for $402K in August 2025. Weighing both, the market-aligned target is the low-$400s.

Market Context — Garfield (15224)
🏘️
Bifurcated Market
Renovated flips trade at $270–$290/sqft while un-renovated shells trade at $80–$155/sqft. Condition, not location, drives most of the price spread.
📍
Location Drivers
Borders Bloomfield, Friendship, Lawrenceville & East Liberty. Strong access to Bakery Square, Children's Hospital, universities, and downtown.
🏢
Investor Demand
Strong rental fundamentals and proximity to Bakery Square, Children's Hospital, and the universities keep investor demand active alongside owner-occupant buyers.
📈
Market Trend
Garfield and Friendship have softened modestly over the trailing 12 months — argues for a confident but not aggressive list price.

A clean, fully renovated home is exactly what this market rewards. The most recent 4BR sales (May 2026) closed at or above asking, confirming active buyer demand in Garfield. Priced at the top of the 3BR comp band with strong photography and a tight launch, 5131 Broad is positioned to attract serious buyers quickly. The risk remains over-pricing past the 3BR band without the bedroom count to justify it — which is what happened to this home in 2022.
